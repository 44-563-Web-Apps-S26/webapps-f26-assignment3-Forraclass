Tricky Tiger and Tyrone

Tricky Tiger was granted the power of flight after falling into a vat of acid after being sent to Earth in a rocketship during the destruction of his home planet while his parents were murdered in an ally in a mugging gone wrong. Tyrone is a plucky terrier that can do back flips.
